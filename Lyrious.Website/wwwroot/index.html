For bands, on stage and at rehearsal

Every song, every chord, the whole band in time.

Lyrious keeps your band's setlists, songbook and lyrics with chords in one place. The lyrics scroll in time with the song, and everyone in the band follows along live, on their own screen.

Log in to Lyrious See what it does

Everything the band needs to play

From the first rehearsal to the last encore: the songs, the order you play them in, and the lyrics and chords right in front of you.

Lyrics that scroll in time

Set the tempo and press play: the lyrics move at the pace of the song, so your hands stay on your instrument.

Setlists for every gig

Put songs in order for tonight's show, reuse them for the next, and step to the next song with one tap.

The band's songbook

All your songs, with lyrics, chords, key, tempo and tags. Import a SongbookPro backup to get started in minutes.

Transpose and capo

Change the key of any song for your voice, or show the chords with a capo. Your own settings, without changing the band's.

Play along with the record

Link a song to its sound file, Spotify or YouTube, and the lyrics follow the music as it plays.

Works offline

No signal backstage? Lyrious keeps your songs on the device and syncs the changes when you're back online.

Play together, live

When one of you picks the next song or presses play, everyone's screen follows. No more shouting song names across the stage.

Create your band

Make an account, start a band and invite the others by email.

Fill the songbook

Write or paste lyrics with chords, or import the songs you already have.

Build tonight's setlist

Pick the songs and put them in order. Everyone in the band sees it at once.

Play

Join the band and turn on band sync: the song, the scrolling and the next song stay the same for everyone.

On the screens you already have

Use Lyrious right in your browser, on a laptop, tablet or phone, and install it as an app from there. Apps for phones, tablets and computers are coming.
